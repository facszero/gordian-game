## Prompt

Twelve isolated full body poses, centered in each cell, ample green margin, no grid or text. Flat pure #00ff00 chroma green background. Reference 1 shows the mechanical nesting anatomy: smaller WHITE BLUE Protesser fits inside RED Dellinger, matching arms and legs. Reference 2 defines our painted cel anime style and red Dellinger identity. Keep proportions, line quality, colors consistent across frames. All Dellinger figures same height and foot baseline, even when arms spread. Red helmet, red chest shoulders forearms shins, white upper arms thighs, silver face, blue eyes. NOT a photo or toy.
